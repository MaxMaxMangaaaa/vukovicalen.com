What Is Framegate Intelligence?

Framegate Intelligence is the commercial strategic advisory and intelligence work founded by Alen Vukovic around political communication in algorithmic media environments. It is separate from the independent research published on vukovicalen.com, although both are informed by overlapping questions about campaigns, media, narratives and political behavior.

What Problem Does Framegate Address?

Political actors operate in media environments where messages are rapidly reframed, redistributed and interpreted across journalism, platforms, podcasts, creators and social networks. This creates a strategic problem: decision-makers often have abundant information but limited orientation.

What Does Framegate Focus On?

Framegate focuses on political communication, campaign strategy, narrative dynamics, media environments and decision support for political challengers and incumbents.

How Is It Different From the Research on This Site?

vukovicalen.com is the research layer. Its purpose is to publish empirical work, explain methods, document evidence and explore research questions.

Framegate is the commercial layer. Its purpose is to apply relevant evidence and analysis to real strategic problems faced by political and institutional decision-makers.

Where Does CampaignConsult.ai Fit?

CampaignConsult.ai is being developed as a campaign intelligence platform for lawmakers and candidates. It is a separate product initiative within the broader commercial ecosystem.

Why Keep Research and Commercial Work Separate?

The separation makes the role of each output clearer. Research should be evaluated on data, methods, reasoning and limitations. Advisory work is evaluated on whether it helps a client make better decisions in a specific context.

The two can inform each other without being presented as the same thing.
